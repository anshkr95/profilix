## 1. Description of Service
Profilix is a lightweight profile aggregator, visualization tool, and auditing dashboard. It provides an interface to view and analyze publicly accessible Reddit feeds and historical community archives (via the Arctic Shift API). 

Profilix does not host, index, or store the content queried. It acts strictly as a real-time, client-side browser client for public internet content.
