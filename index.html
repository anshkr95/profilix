Privacy Policy

At Profilix, we respect your privacy and the privacy of others.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect or store any personal data from our users. All searches are performed anonymously, and data is fetched directly from public sources and archives in real-time.

2. Reddit API Compliance

Profilix operates strictly within the bounds of public data aggregation. We do not violate Reddit's Terms of Service. We do not bypass authentication, scrape private data, or access unauthorized endpoints. The data we retrieve is strictly public information or information that was previously made public and legally archived by third-party services like PullPush.

3. Data Retention

We do not retain the data you search for. Once your browser session is closed, the fetched data is cleared.
